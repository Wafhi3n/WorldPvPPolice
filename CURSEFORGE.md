## Summary

Killed by another player in the open world? Call your guild for help. Guildmates see where you died
and who did it, and can answer "On my way".

## Description

You get ganked on the road to Booty Bay. Under the death window, a small box asks "Call your guild
for help?" and already names who killed you, with their class. If a group camps you, it lists up to
five of them. They're read from the whole death recap, not just the killing blow, so a hunter who
let the pet finish you off still makes the list. Type a message if you want ("two rogues at the crossroads, I'm stuck") and click Call. If you
release your spirit with a message typed, the call goes out as well.

Every guildmate running the addon gets a window: your name, the zone, the coordinates where you
died, how many times you've died, the killers and your message. "On my way" tells the guild they're
coming and puts a waypoint on you. "Show on map" drops a skull on the world map.

You keep a "Your call" window that lists who's on the way. Die again and the same call updates
instead of starting a new one, and you can change the message from there. When it's over, click
"All clear" and the call closes for everyone. A call with no news for 30 minutes closes on its own.

Calls survive a /reload or a logout. A guildmate who logs in while your call is still running gets
it too. Battlegrounds, arenas and dungeons are left alone: the box only shows up in the open world.

Everything goes through the guild's addon channel, so nothing gets posted in guild chat. Don't want
the windows? Type /wpp off (or untick the box in /wpp options) and you'll stop receiving calls. You
can still send your own.

Your guild has a Discord? The optional World PvP Police Guild - Discord addon posts the calls there,
where the World PvP Police bot turns them into cards with the zone map.

Made for WoW: Forever. This is a first beta and it's only been tried on a test client, so live
servers may still surprise it. If something looks wrong, open an issue on GitHub and say what you
saw. English, French, German and Spanish.
